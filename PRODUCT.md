# TRACE — product

**One line.** A reference manager with a bounded AI research assistant, built so a student can defend every claim in a short evidence brief.

**Who uses it.** University students writing a short evidence-based assignment, usually on a laptop, sometimes on a phone. They want the speed of AI but answer personally for every citation. They are not researchers by trade and will not read instructions.

**The job.** Question → approved search plan → real sources (Crossref) → chosen evidence passages → at most five short claims → each claim inspected against its stored passage → student approves → export a brief with APA 7 references and an honest AI-use disclosure.

**What is uniquely true.** A real DOI does not make a claim supported. TRACE keeps five checks apart — reference identity, evidence access, excerpt integrity, AI-suggested support, the student's decision — and never merges them into one "verified" badge. Approvals go stale when content changes. One export gate re-checks everything.

**Mode.** Operate (workspace) and Persuade (welcome screen).

**Success.** A student finishes the journey without help; a judge sees a check fail live and clear only when fixed.

**Never.** Present sample output as live AI. Call a paper fake. Claim plagiarism detection, correctness, approval, or a tamper-proof record. Invent sources, quotes or policy.
